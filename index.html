Frequently Asked Questions

"What if the customers cancel?"

Some will — that's normal in any round. But these are pre-agreed recurring customers, not cold leads. Retention rates are strong because they've already committed before delivery. And anyone who cancels within 60 days is removed from your batch value entirely — you don't pay for them.

"How long does it take?"

Most clients start seeing customers land in the first day after our ads go live. Batch completion depends on your area and order size, but we keep you updated every step of the way via your dashboard.

"What areas do you cover?"

We deliver customers across the UK and Ireland. During your consultation we'll check availability in your specific area and confirm we can run ads there.
